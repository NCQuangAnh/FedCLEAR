## Reading

- **Some clients careless:** CLEAR is never more than 0.42 behind the better NLPrompt on any
  dataset, and ahead on every DTD scenario (+0.9 to +2.4). NLPrompt as published (uniform class
  marginal) is strong on Pets but collapses on DTD (OT precision 0.16: the uniform marginal is
  wrong for a client holding a few classes).
- **Every client noisy, 50%:** CLEAR is the best or tied-best arm on all three datasets.
- **Every client noisy, light noise (25%):** NLPrompt is slightly better.
  - On Pets and Caltech the gaps are small (−0.75 to +0.37).
  - On DTD CLEAR loses 2.5-3.5 points: every client is flagged and repairs, and repairs on a weak
    model are often wrong.
- **Every client noisy, 75% (reported):** MAE and NLPrompt beat CLEAR (Caltech pair 75%: −9.7 vs
  MAE). The global prompt used to relabel is itself corrupted, and the 60% cap leaves noise in.
- **Old DBR-FPL** breaks when everybody is noisy: its baseline assumes some clean clients; CLEAR
  beats it by 8-41 points.

## What the thesis can claim

- CLEAR-FPL matches or beats NLPrompt and MAE when only some clients are noisy (the federated case
  it was designed for) and at moderate noise on every client.
- It also names the noisy clients, which neither competitor does.

Limitations to state: light noise spread over every client on a hard dataset, and extreme noise
(75%).
