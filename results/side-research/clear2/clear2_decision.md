## Cap study

|---|---|---|---|
| Pets | +9.08 | +0.08 | yes |

Verdict: **the cap stays.** It is reported as a limitation, with this study as its ablation.

- On Pets, the cap costs 6.9 points at N_sym75 and 11.3 at N_pair75 (both p ≤ 0.005, 5 of 5 seeds).
- On DTD, removing it hurts at 50%: N_sym50 −2.67 and N_pair50 −3.43. The threshold of 0.1 then touches 39% of correct images.

**Rule 2, threshold sensitivity.**

| | nc (0.1) | nc05 | nc20 |

| Pets, 11-setting mean | 87.05 | 86.68 | 86.76 |
| DTD, 11-setting mean | 47.87 | 49.40 | 45.74 |

**Not a decision.** `nc05` would pass rule 1 on both datasets: Pets +7.85 / −0.10, DTD +1.79 / +0.75. But the pre-registered procedure picks the threshold on Pets, and Pets picks 0.1. Adopting `nc05` now would mean choosing on the test results. It is reported only as an exploratory observation.

## Why v2 loses at 25% (what the records show)

- **Pets:**
  - ρ̂ is biased low by about 0.04–0.05 in every setting, including clean clients (ρ̂ ≈ −0.05).
  - At 25% noise the budget round(ρ̂·n) therefore covers about 80% of the noise.
  - The zero-shot ranking is fixed for the whole run, whereas CLEAR rescores every round with the improving global prompt.
  - Noise caught: v2 69–71%, CLEAR 94–97%.
- **DTD:**
  - The standard error is about 0.15 with roughly 30 images per client, so the detection limit 1.645·se ≈ 0.25 equals the noise rate.
  - About half the clients are therefore not flagged at all.
  - This is the detection limit the theory predicts. P3 put the threshold for "detectable" at the limit itself, which is why P3 fails exactly there.
- **At 75%** v2 has none of these problems. ρ̂ is accurate (error 0.03–0.07), every client is flagged, and v2 reaches 92–96% of the noise against CLEAR's 75–79%. That is where its gains are (Pets +5.9 / +11.7, DTD +1.7 / +3.0).

## What this means for the thesis

- The method stays CLEAR-FPL as run, with the 60% cap, τ = 0.05 and threshold 0.1.
- Its known weakness is the 75% all-noisy setting on Pets (73.5 / 78.4). Both the cap study and v2 show the cause: the cap. A version without it gains 7–12 points there.
- v2 supplies the finding: a client can estimate its own noise rate from zero-shot CLIP alone, within about 0.06 (Pets) to 0.09 (DTD). It does so with no false flags above 5%, and its detection limit is predicted by the theory.
