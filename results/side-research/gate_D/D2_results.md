## Verdict (written after reading the numbers)

4 of 7 checks pass (1, 2b, 3, 5); 3 fail (2a, 4a, 4b).

- **Fails, stated plainly.**
  - 2a: on Caltech het the new method is 0.85 below MAE (p = 0.037). This is the only noisy combo
    where it falls more than 0.5 behind.
  - 4a: on clean DTD it costs 0.87 (p = 0.048) against no defence.
  - 4b: honest client-rounds flagged reach 9% on Pets (clean and noisy) against the 5% limit.
    The 5% limit was badly set: the threshold is the 95th percentile of honest clients in a
    clean run, so about 5% false flags is built in, and the method's own trajectory differs
    from the stage 1 run the threshold came from. On DTD the rate is 3-5% and on Caltech 0%.
- **What holds.**
  - It is the only arm that is never far from the best on any dataset: about equal to MAE on
    Pets and Caltech (except Caltech het), and +5.6 to +6.5 over MAE on every DTD combo (MAE
    breaks on DTD).
  - It is +0.91 over the old DBR-FPL on average.
  - It flags 96-100% of careless client-rounds everywhere.
- **Ablations.** All three components matter, and mostly on DTD, where the global model is weak:
  - repairing every client instead of the flagged ones costs 4-7 points;
  - dropping zero-shot CLIP costs 5-6;
  - the old disagreement score costs 7-9. It flags 83-88% of honest client-rounds on DTD,
    against 3-5% for the new score.

  On Pets and Caltech the ablations are within about 0.5, except het.
